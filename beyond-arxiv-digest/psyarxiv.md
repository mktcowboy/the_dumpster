# Psychology — PsyArXiv Top 20, Sep 18 – Oct 18, 2026 (scout report, received Oct 18, 2026)

## The stats hunt — result: no public ranking source exists
1. OSF API v2 (api.osf.io/v2/preprints/…): preprint and file records carry no download or view fields — verified directly in the JSON attributes.
2. /v2/preprints/{id}/metrics/ and /analytics/: 404.
3. /v2/metrics/ (repo code exists in CenterForOpenScience/osf.io, api/metrics/): 404 on production — code isn't deployed publicly.
4. Detail-page HTML (osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/{id}/): 4KB JS shell, no embedded counters. OSF's own help docs (https://help.osf.io/article/408-preprint-analytics) confirm each preprint has view/download counters, but they render only in the browser app and are not exposed by any public endpoint — corroborated by a peer-review discussion noting "download statistics are not available through the API".
5. Altmetric API: now requires an API key (as of Nov 10, 2025) — blocked without one.
6. PsyArXiv discover page (https://psyarxiv.com/discover): sort options are Relevance-only; no trending/most-downloaded view.
7. OpenAlex / Crossref: fresh DOIs aren't indexed yet (404s), so cited-by counts are nonexistent at this age.

Bottom line: no verifiable download/view/attention ranking for month-fresh PsyArXiv preprints. Anyone claiming a download-ranked top 20 for this window would be making it up.

## Methodology
- Enumerated all 1,222 PsyArXiv records with date_created ≥ 2026-09-18 via the OSF API (13 pages), deduplicated superseded versions (kept latest, e.g. v3gh5_v2, sguq5_v8).
- Selected 20 prioritizing: (a) landmark formats — consensus statements, megastudies, large meta-analyses, preregistered/registered reports; (b) data scale (N, countries); (c) novelty + public relevance (AI, GLP-1, psychedelics policy, PISA, misinformation); (d) team prominence as tiebreaker; (e) subfield diversity enforced — max ~2 per subfield.
- Rank order is editorial impact estimate, not a measured ranking. Every entry real: title, authors, abstract verified against the OSF API; URLs from the API's own html link field.

## The 20
1. [Generative AI use and the 2022–2025 decline in PISA performance across countries](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/asm8r_v2/) — Guerra, Aslanov — Oct 8 — Preregistered country-level analysis suggesting GenAI spread contributed to PISA 2025's record-low scores.
2. [The World Federation of ADHD International Consensus Statement: 2026 Update](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/w5n9t_v1/) — Faraone, Pritchard, Bellgrove, et al. — Oct 10 — The field's consensus statement refreshed with 2021–2025 evidence to fight misconceptions and stigma.
3. [What Fifty Years of Research on the Misinformation Effect Can Tell Us: A Meta-Analytic Review](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/m68kf_v6/) — Edmar, Enquist, Luke — Oct 1 — Three-level meta-regression of the 1975–2025 misinformation-paradigm literature and its key moderators.
4. [Moral Goodness is Feminine](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/sguq5_v8/) — Wylie, Gantman, Mende-Siedlecki, et al. — Oct 6 — N=5,758: mental representations of "a morally good person" are feminized across faces, behaviors, and impressions.
5. [fMRI hyperscanning reveals widespread neural coupling during natural conversations](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/5ygkj_v3/) — Mwilambwe-Tshilobo, Tsoi, Speer, et al. — Oct 9 — Two brains scanned simultaneously align neurally during free-flowing, self-constructed conversation — but not scripted dialogue.
6. [A Megastudy of Behavioral Interventions to Increase Voter Registration Ahead of the 2024 U.S. Presidential Election](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/p6b2e_v5/) — Mason, Okst, Green, et al. — Oct 9 — Ten expert-crowdsourced interventions head-to-head in N=12,927 unregistered voters.
7. [The Psychology of GLP-1 Receptor Agonists: Evidence for Distinct Effects on Reward, Interoception and Cognition](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/vhrka_v2/) — Watson, Rehn, Kendig, et al. — Oct 14 — N=704 users: food cravings and alcohol urges drop through distinct pathways, not one appetite switch.
8. [Beyond Initial Crisis Recognition: Suicide-Risk Responses of ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini Across 14-Turn Ukrainian-Language Conversations](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/v3gh5_v2/) — Kuzyk, Salamon, Andreieva, et al. — Oct 14 — Chatbots mostly spot a crisis at first, then stop mentioning it as the conversation moves on — a safety gap.
9. [Can personality be deliberately changed? Effects of a randomized, placebo-controlled intervention on character strengths and Big Five personality traits](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/c3fas_v3/) — Wagner, Vylobkova, Heintz, et al. — Oct 9 — N=3,434 preregistered RCT: an 8-week character-strength training shifts targeted strengths states vs placebo and waitlist.
10. [Peer Transmission of Mental Health Information Increases ADHD Self-Diagnosis](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/dj6me_v1/) — Katiyar, Gillett, Hunt, et al. — Oct 2 — Cultural-evolution account of how social media's peer-to-peer psychiatric content changes what people see and self-diagnose.
11. [General and domain-specific genetic signals shape educational outcomes in children and adults](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/tpgva_v2/) — Starr, Pool, Davis, et al. — Oct 14 — N=15,669 twin-registry study: measured genomic variation for literacy/numeracy shows overlapping and domain-specific patterns.
12. [Oregon's State-Regulated Psilocybin Services: Early Real-World Safety Signals](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/gauby_v1/) — Luoma, Chwyl, Des Jarlais, et al. — Oct 1 — First population-level safety data from legal supervised psilocybin outside tightly controlled trials.
13. [What Do People Dream About? Themes and Emotions in 400,000+ Dream Reports Shared on Weibo (2018–2023)](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/75tke_v1/) — Lyu, Tang, Liu — Oct 13 — 418,961 spontaneous dream posts mined with NLP — dream research at a scale retrospective surveys can't touch.
14. [Feeling the heat: How climate emotions shape trust in government and policy support across 68 countries](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/32pv6_v1/) — Avellino, Stucki, Ghasemi, et al. — Oct 14 — N=69,534: climate emotions can damage wellbeing yet also drive policy support, with government trust in the middle.
15. [Everyday Encounters with Economic Inequality](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/uemvp_v3/) — Moccia, Easterbrook, Gobel — Oct 15 — Situation-sampling identifies distinct forms of real-world inequality encounters with different political and psychological consequences.
16. [Gender bias across LLMs is common and highly heterogeneous](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/buznf_v1/) — Bolzoni, Capraro — Sep 30 — Ten models, nine vendors: gender bias is widespread but varies sharply across systems — no single "LLM bias" story.
17. [Hyper-Expressions: AI-Generated Faces Emulate and Amplify Stereotypical Human Emotions](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/bz3fr_v2/) — Reshef, Aviezter, Goldstein — Oct 14 — AI-generated emotional faces don't just mimic classic stereotypical expressions — they exaggerate them (N=648).
18. [The psychology of cringe](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/qdwv8_v3/) — Deutchman, Lahiri — Oct 7 — Five studies (N=1,416, three preregistered): cringe is a distinct variety of vicarious embarrassment, measurable as both trait and state.
19. [Generalizable experiment agents threaten online behavioral experiments](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/7k9am_v1/) — Özüdoğru, Chetverikov, Huskey, et al. — Oct 1 — A plain-prompted consumer LLM agent independently completed 26 online experiments across 7 platforms — a methods-integrity alarm.
20. [Do 'tortured phrases' exist in the psychology literature, and are there psychology-specific 'tortured phrases'?](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/th3r2_v1/) — Nebl, McCoy, Teixeira da Silva — Oct 7 — The Problematic Paper Screener's tortured-phrase detector finds 36 suspect psychology journal articles — a paper-mill fingerprint in the field.
