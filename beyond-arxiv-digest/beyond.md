## economics
- [Does AI Assistance Enhance or Erode Expertise? Evidence from a Three-Month Field Experiment in Patent Drafting](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35720) — Autor, Rodchenko, Martin, et al. — NBER w35720, Sep 2026 — Preregistered RCT with 133 patent lawyers: AI lifted drafting quality, but three months on only senior lawyers kept the gain without it. Conducted with Google.
- [Are Prediction Markets Politically Biased?](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35846) — Eric Zitzewitz — NBER w35846, Oct 2026 — Returns from 1880 to 2025 show no partisan overpricing; one of the scout's SSRN near-misses, featured in an a16z newsletter.
- [From Importer to Exporter: Oil Shocks and the U.S. Economy](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35864) — Känzig, Stock, Zanotti — NBER w35864, 2026 — Oil supply shocks have turned from contractionary to expansionary for the U.S. as it became a net exporter.
- [Debt Sustainability and the Range of Multiple Equilibria](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35850) — Blanchard, Martínez-Bruera, Huertas — NBER w35850, 2026 — More uncertainty shrinks, then eliminates, the range of self-fulfilling sovereign-debt equilibria.
