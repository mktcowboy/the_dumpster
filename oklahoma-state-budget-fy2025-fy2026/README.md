## Headlines

- FY2025 (SB1125): **$12.47B** headline; GA bill only $11.558B. Largest nominal budget ever at the time; agencies got ~2% increases.
- FY2026 (HB2766): **$12.59B** headline (+0.56% reported); GA bill only $11.593B (+0.30%). Became law **without the governor's signature** (5/29/2025).
- Education ~49.7% of the GA bill ($5.758B); Health +4.1% to $2.322B (OHCA $1.311B → $1.411B, +7.6%). Declines were overwhelmingly one-time roll-offs, not base cuts.
- Signature tax changes: FY2025 repealed the state **grocery sales tax** (HB1955, −$418M/yr recurring); FY2026 enacted a **0.25% income-tax cut** with elimination triggers (HB2764, −$340M/yr full effect), backfilled with one-time savings.
- FY2026 one-time mega-projects: Lawton prison purchase **$312M**, OSU vet hospital **$250M**, OU pediatric heart hospital **$200M**.

## Fiscal-year convention

Oklahoma FY runs July 1 – June 30. FY2025 = July 2024–June 2025; FY2026 = July 2025–June 2026.

## Caveats

- Figures are as published by the sources (mostly index-fetched official documents, one OMES page read live); not re-verified against live budget systems.
- Different sources report different top-line totals depending on what's counted (GA bill only vs GA + supplementals vs GA + one-time transfers). See `topline-reconciliation.csv` and pick one basis per analysis; do not mix.
- Open questions are flagged at the end of report.md (e.g., the exact FY2025 base behind the reported +0.56%).
