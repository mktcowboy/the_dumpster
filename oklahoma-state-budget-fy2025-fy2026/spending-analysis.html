Big picture: nearly flat, but uneven

Comparable GA-bill change, FY25 → FY26

Health and education account for most growth. Together they rose , more than the total GA-bill increase because cuts elsewhere offset them.
Large declines are mainly one-time roll-offs. General government/transportation and natural resources/regulatory fell most sharply, reflecting expiring capital and program allocations.
FY26 still contains major one-time projects. The prison purchase, OSU vet hospital, and OU pediatric heart hospital together total $762M.
Tax changes add recurring pressure. The grocery-tax repeal and the income-tax cut reduce recurring revenue while lawmakers used one-time resources.
