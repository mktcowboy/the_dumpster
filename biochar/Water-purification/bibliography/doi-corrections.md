# DOI corrections for the work-repository top-20 list

The papers audit (its DOI-verification table) records three bad identifiers in the work repository's top-20 list of biochar and activated-carbon papers. That list is not in this dumpster folder. The corrections are copied here so they are on hand when the list is updated in the work repository.

This note does not add any other DOI changes. The shortlist CSV already uses the corrected Bhatnagar DOI. It does not include the Rodríguez-Reinoso or Radovic items.

| Item | Listed identifier | Problem recorded in the audit | Correct DOI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rodríguez-Reinoso (audit item 15) | `10.1016/S0008-6223(97)00190-5` | Resolves to Rubio et al. on SO2 flue-gas chars, not the Rodríguez-Reinoso catalysis paper. | [https://doi.org/10.1016/S0008-6223(97)00173-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0008-6223(97)00173-5) |
| Bhatnagar (audit item 19) | `10.1016/j.cej.2013.01.073` | Resolves to Tao et al. on methane reforming, not the Bhatnagar review of activated-carbon modification for water treatment. | [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2012.12.038](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2012.12.038) |
| Radovic chapter (audit item 18) | Taylor & Francis chapter link | The audit reports that the Taylor & Francis chapter link is a 404 in the summaries note. | [https://doi.org/10.1201/9781482270129-11](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781482270129-11) |

Cite the corrected DOIs. Do not cite the listed Rodríguez-Reinoso or Bhatnagar DOIs for those papers.
