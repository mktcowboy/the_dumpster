Methodology & caveats

  • Data gathered October 19, 2026 via place-search sweeps across 150+ Oklahoma cities plus cross-checks against national storage directories.
  • A few records carry rebrand or possible-duplicate flags — these are shown verbatim in the profile notes, unresolved.
  • Many records lack zip, phone, or website where the source did not publish them; blanks are gaps, not negatives.
  • 18 small towns were verified with zero named facilities and are excluded from this directory.
  • Tulsa’s south corridor (101st St / Jenks border) may be slightly undercounted.
  • Coordinates are geocoded approximations, not survey data. Records marked “approximate location” resolved only to their city centroid.
