02 · The founders

Jon & Andrea Allen

The couple met behind the bar at Arsaga's in Fayetteville. They still run Onyx themselves.

Barista since 2002
  • 2020 U.S. Barista Champion
  • 2nd at the 2021 World Barista Championship in Milan, the best U.S. finish since 2015
  • Her world-stage routine used Colombian coffees and was about kindness after the pandemic
Co-founder & CEO

Jon Allen

Two decades in coffee
  • Leads the business side: sourcing, wholesale, retail expansion
  • Co-designed The Preserve café with architect Marlon Blackwell
  • Thinks the most sustainable coffee is high-end specialty coffee
Ownership
Founder-owned

Mantra

The audience
Coffee people first

Jon has described Onyx as a company whose basic audience was the coffee industry itself.

06 · Business model

The competition flywheel

Onyx uses barista championships as marketing, and the attention they bring pays for its expensive sourcing.

Sourcing

154 producers. Onyx publishes the price it paid on every coffee.

Roasting

Solar-powered infrared roasters in Rogers. Every batch is tested and cupped.

Wholesale

Coffee plus training for cafés. Lance Hedrick runs it from Portugal.

Cafés

Designed showrooms that also work as marketing for the brand.

12 · Outlook

Strengths, risks, and what's next

This section is my analysis, based on the reporting cited below.

Strengths

  • Award-winning staff give it credibility other roasters can't buy
  • Its price transparency is hard for rivals to copy
  • It controls everything from green buying to the cup
  • With no investors, it can grow at its own pace

Weaknesses

  • All 8 cafés are in one metro area
  • Its $21–25 bags are premium-priced at a time when shoppers are watching costs
  • Growing without investors limits how fast it can expand
  • The brand depends on a few people: the founders and its star staff

Opportunities

  • Europe, through its partnership with Manhattan Coffee Roasters in the Netherlands
  • Full-service hospitality like The Preserve, with higher spend per visit
  • New product lines: Circadian, Terroir chocolate, advent calendars
  • Being world #1 should help win wholesale accounts

Threats

  • The commodity C price above $4/lb squeezes every roaster's margins
  • Coffee import tariffs and freight costs
  • Rival roasters hiring away its trained baristas and trainers
  • Fast café openings could stretch quality control

What to watch in 2027

  1. Whether the Drake Farms café (4,780 sq ft) opens and what happens to the Gregg Street shop
  2. Whether the European business expands beyond its first partnership
  3. Whether Onyx opens a café outside Arkansas, which it has never done
  4. Whether it keeps a top ranking on the 2027 World's 100 Best list
