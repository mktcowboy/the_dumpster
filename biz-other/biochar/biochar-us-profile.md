# US Biochar Industry Profile (as of Oct 22, 2026)

**Bottom line:** US biochar is still a small industry, worth about **$157M in 2025** with about **151,000 t** produced, according to the [ABI 2025 US Market Report](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf). It's growing fast, and carbon-removal (CDR) credits fund most of that growth. Physical biochar mostly goes into agriculture at a few hundred dollars a ton. Credits trade around **$150/t CO2e**, so for many producers the credit is worth about as much as the char, or more. Demand for credits is concentrated in a few buyers and has wobbled in 2026. The fact that matters most for CaneCycle: **a bagasse biochar plant already runs at Cora Texas in White Castle, LA** (American BioCarbon) and is scaling up, so CaneCycle's differentiation has to come from engineered water-treatment media, not from commodity soil char.

## 1. Market size
- **[ABI survey](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf) (2025, US industry revenue):** $157M, of which producers took $58.8M. That's a 71% CAGR since 2023, with $442M projected for 2027.
- **[Grand View](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/biochar-market/united-states) (2025, US):** $222.8M, growing at a 13% CAGR to $589M by 2033.
- **[IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/united-states-biochar-market) (2025, US):** $602.2M, growing at a 10.5% CAGR to $1.51B by 2034.

These estimates differ by about 4×. ABI's is the only one built bottom-up from producer surveys. ABI puts 2025 production at 151,329 t, up from 86,893 t in 2023. ABI also notes that producers' projection of 819,000 t by 2027 is "unlikely." A [CarbonCredits.com summary](https://carboncredits.com/u-s-biochar-industry-triples-to-157-million-as-carbon-removal-market-sparks-record-growth/) of the same report gives a different figure, ">97,000 t" produced with >400,000 t of installed capacity, so treat production volumes as uncertain.

Respondents ranked **biochar fertilizers (34%) and water/stormwater filtration (30%)** as the top priority markets to develop ([ABI](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf)).

**The CDR market:** Biochar made up 80% of delivered durable CDR in 2025 ([CDR.fyi](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/durable-cdr-market-update-carbon-unbound-east-coast-2026)). It has 290+ buyers, but Microsoft alone accounts for 46% of all biochar tonnes contracted ([CDR.fyi 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)). Suppliers say they need **$187/t** for a reasonable profit, while buyers already call $155 "expensive" ([CDR.fyi survey](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/cdr-pricing-survey-jan-2025)). The main registries are **Puro.earth, Isometric, CSI Global C-Sink, and Verra**. US deals include Microsoft–**Liferaft** (1M units over 10 years, IA/IL), Microsoft–**Carba** (44k, MN), Microsoft–**Pacific Biochar** (CA), and Google–**Charm** (100k t). I couldn't find a US biochar offtake in Frontier's portfolio (its Charm contract is for bio-oil). Frontier's Louisiana deal is with **Arbor**, a BECCS project in Lake Charles, not biochar ([Frontier](https://frontierclimate.com/portfolio/arbor)).

## 3. Sales and distribution
Distributors report selling mainly direct to end users (80%), then online (47%), through wholesalers (33%), via ag suppliers (27%), and through home and garden stores (27%). Buyers care most about carbon content, particle size, and feedstock ([ABI](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf)). Forty-four percent of producers sell **uncertified** biochar. The rest hold USDA BioPreferred (29%), OMRI (25%), WBC (12%), or EBC (10%). In carbon credits, 85% of Puro biochar CORCs sell through brokers or platforms rather than directly to buyers ([Puro](https://puro.earth/insights/post/346-biochars-market-leadership-in-carbon-removal/)). Main brands include Wakefield (big-box retail), Pacific Biochar's Blacklite (bulk), and Oregon Biochar Solutions.

## 4. Production
Woody biomass is the dominant feedstock, and continuous auger or rotary-kiln systems make up 43% of reported production systems. High-carbon fly ash from biomass boilers is the largest single source by volume, but its share is shrinking ([ABI](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf)).

## 5. Policy and standards
- **Certification:** The IBI certification program was **retired in 2024** and merged into CSI's World Biochar Certificate. EBC remains the European standard ([IBI](https://biochar-international.org/news/driving-confidence-in-biochar-ibi-and-csi-merge-standards-for-industry-growth/)).
- **NRCS CPS 336 (soil carbon amendment):** Biochar must be pyrolyzed above 350 °C with H:Corg <0.7. Biosolids-based char is excluded, and so are field residues that should stay on the land. The base scenario is 4 yd³/acre. Payment rates vary by state, from $102/ac in MO (FY24) to $1,427/ac in PA (FY26) ([NRCS FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf), [PA](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/PA_Bulletin%20440-26-06%20PA%20Published%20Cost%20List%20and%20Payment%20caps%20for%20FY%2026%20EQIP%20%20CSP_edited.pdf)). **I couldn't find Louisiana's FY26 rate.** Pull it from the [LA payment schedule](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/state-offices/louisiana/payment-schedule).
- **Federal:** No biochar tax credit exists. A House-passed biochar demonstration program would cover up to 35% of capital costs but is **not law** ([B&D, Jun 2025](https://www.bdlaw.com/publications/congress-eyes-sweeping-changes-to-carbon-and-biomass-incentives-45q-tax-credit-reforms-new-biochar-program-and-forest-residues-carbon-credit-introduced/)). 45Q is built for captured CO2 and doesn't readily cover biochar.
- **States:** California's Healthy Soils Program added biochar as a practice. California's SB 88 biochar strategy bill was **vetoed** in Oct 2025 ([CARB](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/2025-senate-bill-88-caballero-anna-air-resources-carbon-emissions-biomass-vetoed)).
- **Louisiana:** There's no biochar-specific statute. The LSU AgCenter has a [sugarcane biochar BMP](https://www.lsuagcenter.com/profiles/astrahan/articles/page1697731513153) recommending 1–2 t/acre in the fallow year. Robinson's project shows that local **solid-waste revenue bonds** can serve as a financing route.

## 6. Risks and economics
- **Buyer concentration:** As of April 2026, Microsoft had bought 78.5% of all disclosed durable CDR tonnes. It reportedly paused new purchases in April 2026, then signed new deals, including a 650k t deal in May ([CDR.fyi](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/durable-cdr-market-update-carbon-unbound-east-coast-2026), [ESG Dive](https://www.esgdive.com/news/microsoft-buys-first-carbon-credits-reportedly-pausing-purchases/820885/)).
- **Delivery risk:** In 2025, biochar suppliers delivered 54% less than they projected, and 73% of projects revised their capacity down ([Supercritical](https://25023219.fs1.hubspotusercontent-eu1.net/hubfs/25023219/Marketing/2026%20Supercritical%20Delivery%20Risk%20Report.pdf)). Buyers now pay a premium for suppliers with a track record.
- **Credit dependence:** Producers in the 71–80% credit-revenue bracket account for 25% of US output ([ABI](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf)). Forward prices sit at a small discount to spot because more supply is expected ([Platts](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/111325-biochar-carbon-credit-prices-steady-but-sentiment-weakens)). Possible oversupply from India is a concern for later years.
- **Physical demand:** Producers rank weak demand for physical biochar and the lack of product specs as their #2 and #3 obstacles to growth. Freight is also a major cost: plants typically draw feedstock from within about 100 miles, and it takes 4–5 t of feedstock to make 1 t of biochar ([WSU](https://wpcdn.web.wsu.edu/cahnrs/uploads/sites/44/Biomass2Biochar-Chapter6.pdf), [USBI](https://nacwconference.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/NACW-2024-Scaling-Biochar-State-of-the-Industry-and-Opportunities-for-Growth.pdf)).

## 7. So what for CaneCycle
1. **Talk to American BioCarbon early, as a possible partner or competitor.** It already holds the Cora Texas lease, Puro certification, an OMRI listing, and an oilfield sorbent angle. Find out which of Louisiana's [11 mills](https://www.amscl.org/education/raw-sugar-factories/) still have uncommitted bagasse.
2. **Model credits as a revenue line.** American BioCarbon's audited conversion factor is 1.122 t CO2e per t of dry bagasse char ([Puro audit](https://puro.earth/documents/content/3kUhDbgMSgWhwHwS1U5zoQo18ee)). At about $150/t, that's roughly $170 in credit revenue per tonne of char (my calculation). That's comparable to bulk soil-char prices, but you have to plan for buyer concentration and delivery-risk premiums.
3. **Water media is the high-value gap.** Activated carbon costs about $3,000+/t, and producers name filtration as a priority market. Bagasse char's ash content and consistency need to meet filtration specs, such as <5% ash for metals removal.
4. **Use NRCS 336 and the LSU BMP to sell soil char locally.** Confirm that mill bagasse counts as processing residue rather than field residue, and get Louisiana's FY26 payment rate.
5. **Get certified from the start.** Use WBC or EBC for the material and Puro or Isometric for credits. A delivery track record is now what buyers are paying for.
6. **Look at Louisiana bond financing.** The EEIDD/Robinson structure is a working example.

## Appendix A: Additional detail
- ABI producer revenue: $18.2M (2023) → $58.8M (2025) → $159M projected (2027). Producers deriving 11–20% of revenue from credits account for 42% of US production; 6% of production has no credit link.
- Value-add practices among producers: biochar-enhanced fertilizers 39%, potting media 26%, **water filtration blends 26%**, concrete admixtures 20%, **activated-carbon replacement products 20%**, specialty feed additives 11%, asphalt 9%. Only 22% add no value ([ABI](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf)).
- Pygas use: 39% dry feedstock with it, 32% use it for process heat, 26% for electricity, 21% flare it unused.
- Global context: the 2023 global survey found ≥352,304 t produced ([IBI/USBI 2023](https://biochar-international.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Global-Biochar-Market-Report-2023-%E2%80%93-Public.pdf)). Biochar CDR market value was $14.6M (2022), $33.9M (2023), and $181.5M (2024) ([CDR.fyi](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)).
- Credit sales H1 2026 vs H1 2025: 2.99Mt vs 1.59Mt, with spot volumes down 54% and offtakes up 106%. 81% of "high-quality" supply was committed by July 2026 (Supercritical's own rating, and Supercritical is a broker) ([Supercritical](https://biochar.gosupercritical.com/)).
- CDR.fyi survey: biochar's average breakeven is $143/t, and most suppliers fall in the $100–200 range. Veteran suppliers report *higher* breakevens than new entrants.
- Other price points: credits certified under CSI's Global C-Sink trade at a discount to Puro and Isometric; US Isometric biochar was offered at $170/t for vintage 2025 ([Platts](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/111325-biochar-carbon-credit-prices-steady-but-sentiment-weakens)).
- Feedstock cost is typically up to $75/t, and "difficult" feedstocks can earn tipping fees ([USBI 2024](https://nacwconference.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/NACW-2024-Scaling-Biochar-State-of-the-Industry-and-Opportunities-for-Growth.pdf)).
- Louisiana sugar: 11 mills; record ~18M t of cane ground for the 2025 crop (per [AMSCL](https://www.amscl.org/sugar-news/a-look-behind-the-records-set-this-harvest-season/) search summary; **verify exact figure**).
- Gulf South pipeline: SunnyDay Renewables in Wellington, FL plans 25–30k t/yr (BET PRD8000, $30M) ([Biochar Today, Jan 2026](https://biochartoday.com/news/sunnyday-renewables-and-biomass-energy-techniques-implement-high-efficiency-pyrolysis-for-carbon-sequestration/)). Applied Carbon in TX claims "hundreds of tons per week" (LinkedIn post, low confidence).
- Biosolids char (Bioforcetech in Redding, CA and Ephrata, PA; Aries in Linden, NJ) is a separate niche that PFAS concerns shape. It is excluded from NRCS 336.
