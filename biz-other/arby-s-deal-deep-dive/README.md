# Arby's Deal Deep Dive

Sourced deep dive into the Arby's acquisition and turnaround, with a deep dive on Bob Berlin.

Key correction: LongRange Capital never bought Arby's. Roark Capital Group bought Arby's in 2011 (about $430m headline value, $130m cash). LongRange, founded in late 2019 by Bob Berlin, is linked only through Berlin's earlier Baupost-era Arby's role. LongRange's actual restaurant bet is Pizza Hut ex-China, closed September 1, 2026 for about $1.5bn.
