# Rent-Seeking Report

**Date:** October 19, 2026

A report on rent-seeking: what economic rent is, a short history of how rents
have been captured (feudal dues, enclosures, monopoly charters, railroad land
grants, taxi medallions, zoning, platform fees), and the **top 50 ways to
create rental income today** — grouped into real estate, paper/financial
assets, IP & digital, business & equipment, and land & natural resources —
each rated by capital required and effort, plus principles separating durable
rents from fragile ones.
