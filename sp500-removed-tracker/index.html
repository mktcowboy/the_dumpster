Data sources & methodology

1
Removal log — Wikipedia, “Historical components of the S&P 500”. Live article · Permanent revision (Oct 8, 2026) · retrieved Oct 14, 2026. 362 distinct tickers removed, Jan 2007–Oct 2026. Each removal links to its own citation where the Wikipedia row had one (mostly S&P Dow Jones Indices announcements).
2
Prices — monthly closes for each removed ticker plus SPY, Yahoo Finance, retrieved Oct 14, 2026. 164 tickers have history; 198 delisted (acquired/bankrupt) tickers have none and show the removal record only.
3
Corrections applied Oct 14, 2026 — added the missing 2010-02-16 swap: Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.B) replaced Burlington Northern Santa Fe (BNI), which Berkshire acquired — Reuters, Jan 26 2010; fixed ticker artifacts on the 2013-12-02 Allegion/JCPenney row.
4
Method. Returns are price returns (exclude dividends), rebased to 100 on the first month-end on/after the removal date, running to the latest available price. Annualized = geometric mean over elapsed months. “Still trading” means price data reaches Sep 2026 or later.

Limitations. Wikipedia’s table is community-maintained and thinner for 2007–2010. Completeness was audited against current constituents’ “Date added” values: all apparent misses resolved as ticker renames or erroneous dates; the GE/Walgreens 2018 swap was Dow Jones Industrial Average only.
