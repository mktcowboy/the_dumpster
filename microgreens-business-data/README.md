# Microgreens Business Data

USDA-free research dossier for a rack-based commercial microgreens operation
in the OKC / Cleveland County, Oklahoma area (researched Oct 19, 2026).

- `microgreens-business-data.html` — the full report: market size (six firms'
  conflicting 2025 estimates, charted), pricing benchmarks including the OKC
  incumbent's $26/flat, per-tray unit economics with Oklahoma electricity
  rates, startup capex tiers, the vertical-farm shakeout vs. small-grower
  strategy, Oklahoma licensing/tax/zoning rules, named Norman/OKC restaurant
  targets, sales-channel economics, equipment and input prices, eight failure
  modes, profitability benchmarks, and a working per-tray economics calculator.
  Every figure is linked to its source and tagged with a reliability flag
  (study / vendor / anecdote / market-research estimate / author's calc).
  A dedicated Open Questions section states what could not be verified:
  no wholesale price time series, no account churn data, no audited
  small-grower P&Ls.

Notes on sourcing: no USDA sources were used. Two USDA-funded sources were
deliberately excluded during research and are flagged in the page.
Vendor prices were index-sourced — confirm on vendor sites before buying.
