# Solar Farm Proforma — Wichita Falls, TX

Interactive 30-year financial proforma for a hypothetical 1,000-acre greenfield
utility-scale solar farm near Wichita Falls, Texas (ERCOT/Oncor), built
October 12, 2026.

- `solar-farm-proforma.html` — self-contained page: explainer on solar farm
  economics plus the proforma with editable assumptions (sliders), cash-flow
  chart, PPA-price sensitivity table, full 30-year table, and a CSV download.

Base case (all figures from the page, sourced on the page):
- 149 MWdc single-axis tracking (~115 MWac)
- $169.4M all-in capex, $14.7M year-1 revenue, $52/MWh PPA base
- 4.2% unlevered / 2.0% levered IRR, 1.46x year-1 DSCR
- Thinner than an Oklahoma (SPP) equivalent: ERCOT has the cheapest ISO solar
  PPA prices in North America and Texas property tax bites after the 10-year
  Chapter 312 PILOT, despite better sun and faster/cheaper ERCOT
  interconnection.

Key sources embedded on the page: Wood Mackenzie/SEIA (Q2 2026 capex via
pv magazine), LevelTen PPA index, NREL, Berkeley Lab, Global Solar Atlas,
Oklahoma State University extension (land lease rates), IRS/SEIA (ITC/PTC),
SPP and ERCOT market data.

Open questions flagged on the page: ERCOT-specific $/kW interconnection
benchmark, exact Wichita County millage post-PILOT, Texas GRID fee items.
