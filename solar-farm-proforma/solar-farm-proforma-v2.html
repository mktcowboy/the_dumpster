What V2 includes—and deliberately leaves out

Included: land-to-capacity conversion, EPC, a separate grid allowance, development contingency, production decline, curtailment, PPA revenue, a bundled operating-cost allowance, terminal decommissioning, a transferable-ITC proxy, and simple level-pay debt.

Not included: income taxes or bonus depreciation, MACRS, NOLs, DSCR sculpting, property-tax abatement structure, inverter replacement, reserve accounts, tax equity, basis risk, negative-price hours, and detailed merchant-price forecasting. These matter for an investment decision; V1 retains them for diligence.

Most important next diligence: signed PPA terms and escalation, interconnection study and schedule, local property-tax/abatement agreement, yield/P50-P90 report, EPC bid, lender term sheet, and tax-credit eligibility/timing.

How this improves on V1: decision-first output, 12 rather than 28 controls, a single clearly labeled tax-credit treatment, simple level debt, and a two-variable stress table. V1 remains the detailed audit model: open V1.
