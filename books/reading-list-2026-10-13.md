## Part 2: 100 Recommendations

### 1. Media, orality, language
1. *The Gutenberg Galaxy* — McLuhan: print's effect on cognition; Ong's direct intellectual source
2. *Understanding Media* — McLuhan: the framework Meyrowitz extends
3. *Amusing Ourselves to Death* — Postman: how medium shapes discourse (cultural analysis, not partisan)
4. *The Presence of the Word* — Ong: sound, voice, and consciousness; precursor to *Orality and Literacy*
5. *The Muse Learns to Write* — Havelock: the transition from oral to literate Greece, after *Preface to Plato*
6. *The Singer of Tales* — Lord: the oral-formulaic research that underpins Havelock and Ong
7. *Mimesis* — Auerbach: how Western literature represents reality, from Homer onward
8. *The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind* — Jaynes: speculative, but built on orality and the Iliad

### 2. Mind, brain, consciousness
9. *The Matter with Things* — McGilchrist: the full-scale extension of *The Master and His Emissary*
10. *Gödel, Escher, Bach* — Hofstadter: self-reference, formal systems, and mind
11. *The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat* — Sacks: neurological case studies as narrative
12. *Phantoms in the Brain* — Ramachandran: hemispheric and perceptual anomalies
13. *The Embodied Mind* — Varela, Thompson, Rosch: cognition as enacted, not computed
14. *Flow* — Csikszentmihalyi: the psychology of absorbed performance (relevant to Twight and Gonzales)
15. *The Denial of Death* — Becker: mortality as the organizing force of behavior
16. *The Varieties of Religious Experience* — William James: empirical treatment of mystical states

### 3. Religion, Judaism, spirituality
17. *The Sabbath* — Heschel: Jewish time-consciousness; close companion to Gelernter
18. *God in Search of Man* — Heschel: Jewish philosophy of religion
19. *I and Thou* — Buber: dialogic relation as ontology
20. *The Lonely Man of Faith* — Soloveitchik: the tension between mastery and covenant, which parallels McGilchrist's divided brain
21. *Night* — Wiesel: testimony at short length
22. *Confessions* — Augustine: the first sustained interior self-examination
23. *Fear and Trembling* — Kierkegaard: Abraham and the limits of ethical reasoning
24. *Gravity and Grace* — Weil: aphoristic mysticism

### 4. Jewish-American and comic-tragic fiction (Roth lineage)
25. *Herzog* — Bellow: intellectual crisis rendered as farce
26. *The Adventures of Augie March* — Bellow: a voice-driven picaresque
27. *American Pastoral* — Roth: collapse of a constructed life
28. *Sabbath's Theater* — Roth: transgressive, and a tonal match for *Portnoy*
29. *The Assistant* — Malamud: moral weight in a small setting
30. *Call It Sleep* — Henry Roth: immigrant consciousness in stream-of-consciousness prose, structurally close to Joyce's *Portrait*

### 5. Sea and quest narratives
31. *Lord Jim* — Conrad: guilt, honor, and narrative unreliability
32. *Billy Budd* — Melville: compact moral allegory
33. *The Confidence-Man* — Melville: Melville's most opaque work, and a natural follow-up to *Moby-Dick*
34. *Two Years Before the Mast* — Dana: seamanship documented first-hand
35. *In the Heart of the Sea* — Philbrick: the true event behind *Moby-Dick*
36. *Master and Commander* — O'Brian: dense period detail and character-driven voyage
37. *The Odyssey* (Fagles) — Homer: an oral epic that fits the Ong/Havelock cluster

### 6. American mythic and violent prose
38. *All the Pretty Horses* — McCarthy: lyrical frontier prose
39. *The Road* — McCarthy: McCarthy at minimum length
40. *Suttree* — McCarthy: his most Joycean and comic work
41. *Lonesome Dove* — McMurtry: the Western at epic scale with a humane tone
42. *Butcher's Crossing* — John Williams: anti-romantic buffalo hunt, close to *Blood Meridian* in subject
43. *Train Dreams* — Denis Johnson: novella of mythic compression
44. *As I Lay Dying* — Faulkner: polyphonic voices
45. *Absalom, Absalom!* — Faulkner: narration as reconstruction

### 7. Mountaineering, survival, endurance
46. *Annapurna* — Maurice Herzog: the foundational 8,000-meter account
47. *Touching the Void* — Simpson: survival and decision-making under extremity
48. *The Climb* — Boukreev and DeWalt: a counter-narrative to the 1996 Everest accounts
49. *The White Spider* — Harrer: the Eiger North Face
50. *The Mountains of My Life* — Bonatti: alpinist philosophy
51. *The Worst Journey in the World* — Cherry-Garrard: Antarctic endurance in literary prose
52. *South* — Shackleton: leadership and survival
53. *Alive* — Read: the Andes crash, and a case study for *Deep Survival*

### 8. War and combat
54. *A Rumor of War* — Caputo: Vietnam memoir
55. *Dispatches* — Herr: impressionistic combat reportage
56. *The Things They Carried* — O'Brien: fiction and memory entangled
57. *Matterhorn* — Marlantes: infantry novel written from lived experience
58. *With the Old Breed* — Sledge: Pacific infantry memoir with clear-eyed detail
59. *Storm of Steel* — Jünger: WWI as direct experience
60. *The Thin Red Line* — Jones: war from a soldier's interior
61. *On Killing* — Grossman: the psychology of combat

### 9. Probability, math, markets
62. *Fooled by Randomness* — Taleb: uncertainty and misjudged skill
63. *The Black Swan* — Taleb: fat tails and model risk
64. *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* — Lefèvre: trader psychology in narrative form
65. *The Misbehavior of Markets* — Mandelbrot: fractals and non-Gaussian price behavior
66. *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science* — E.T. Jaynes: Bayesian foundations
67. *Stochastic Differential Equations* — Øksendal: the standard applied companion to Le Gall
68. *The Man Who Solved the Market* — Zuckerman: Renaissance Technologies narrative

### 10. Dense, maximalist, philosophical novels
69. *Ulysses* — Joyce: the natural next step after *Portrait*
70. *Gravity's Rainbow* — Pynchon: encyclopedic postmodern density, comparable to *Infinite Jest*
71. *The Recognitions* — Gaddis: a vast novel on forgery and authenticity
72. *The Brothers Karamazov* — Dostoevsky: the culminating Dostoevsky novel
73. *Notes from Underground* — Dostoevsky: the shorter model for the unreliable narrator
74. *The Trial* — Kafka: existential procedural dread
75. *The Magic Mountain* — Mann: ideas in dialogue within a closed setting
76. *2666* — Bolaño: the closest modern analogue to *Blood Meridian*'s dread

### 11. Speculative fiction (Wolfe-adjacent)
77. *The Fifth Head of Cerberus* — Wolfe: identity and unreliable narration, shorter than *New Sun*
78. *Peace* — Wolfe: memory-based narrative puzzle
79. *Soldier of the Mist* (Latro) — Wolfe: amnesiac narrator in ancient Greece
80. *Solaris* — Lem: the limits of comprehension
81. *Roadside Picnic* — Strugatsky: an alien incursion as an unexplained phenomenon
82. *A Canticle for Leibowitz* — Miller: cyclical civilization, with religion at its center
83. *Riddley Walker* — Hoban: invented dialect, with an oral-culture feel
84. *Dune* — Herbert: ecology, religion, and prescience

### 12. Ancient and philosophical texts
85. *The Iliad* — Homer: the oral-formulaic epic at the core of Havelock
86. *The Theban Plays* — Sophocles: tragic structure
87. *Nicomachean Ethics* — Aristotle: virtue and practical judgment
88. *Poetics* — Aristotle: the earliest systematic theory of narrative
89. *Discourses and Enchiridion* — Epictetus: practical Stoicism, a companion to *Meditations*
90. *Letters from a Stoic* — Seneca: the epistolary form of Stoic practice
91. *The Epic of Gilgamesh* — anonymous: the oldest surviving epic
92. *Tao Te Ching* — attributed to Laozi: counterpoint to Western rationalist frameworks

### 13. Transgressive and interior-life fiction
93. *Under the Volcano* — Lowry: alcoholic disintegration in prose
94. *Jesus' Son* — Denis Johnson: linked stories of addiction
95. *Last Exit to Brooklyn* — Selby: more Selby, in the same register as *Requiem*
96. *Journey to the End of the Night* — Céline: bleak, voice-driven picaresque
97. *Hunger* — Hamsun: a starving narrator's psychology
98. *Steppenwolf* — Hesse: a divided self
99. *The Stranger* — Camus: a compact companion to *The Plague*
100. *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas* — Thompson: stylistic excess and moral collapse
