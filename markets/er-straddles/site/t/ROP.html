yfinance is a free, unofficial Yahoo Finance scraper: option quotes are delayed (~15 min) and after hours bid/ask can be stale or zero (we fall back to last trade). Earnings dates/times for future events are often estimates; 'UNK' timing is assumed AMC. There are no historical option prices in yfinance, so past implied moves cannot be reconstructed: the backtest applies today's implied move to every past quarter, and the P&L ignores residual time value, bid-ask, commissions and assignment. The full straddle includes non-earnings time value up to expiry, so the 'ER-only' columns (term-structure variance difference) are the fairer comparison.
