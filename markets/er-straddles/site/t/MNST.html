Cost-aware P&L model. σex = ATM IV of the expiry before earnings (fallback: 63-day realized vol excluding earnings days, flagged 'proxy'). Earnings-day variance E² = σpost²·Tpost − σex²·(Tpost − 1 day). Entry: sell the ATM straddle at the close before the event, valued with variance E² + σex²·Trem (Trem = trading days from reaction-day close to expiry). Exit: buy back at the reaction-day close: Black-Scholes straddle with spot moved by the historical close-to-close move, same strike, σex, Trem (= the residual time value; 0 if expiry is the reaction day). Gross = entry − exit. Net = gross − entry slippage (0.5× half-spread) − exit slippage (0.5× half-spread × 1.0) − commissions ($0.65/contract × 2 legs × open+close). Spread = today's actual call+put bid/ask width for the post-earnings expiry (default 10.0% of mid if unknown). Worst 25% = mean of the worst quarter of outcomes. Settings: config.json.

Liquidity grades: A: OI≥5000, vol≥1000, spread≤5.0%; B: OI≥1000, vol≥200, spread≤10.0%; C: OI≥250, vol≥25, spread≤20.0%; else F. yfinance is a free, unofficial Yahoo Finance scraper: option quotes are delayed (~15 min) and after hours bid/ask can be stale or zero (we fall back to last trade). Earnings dates are cross-checked against Nasdaq's earnings calendar, but both can be estimates until the company announces. There are no historical option prices in yfinance, so past implied moves cannot be reconstructed: the backtest applies today's term structure to every past quarter.
