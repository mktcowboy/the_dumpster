Method & limits

How the numbers were built.

Max pain is the strike that minimizes the aggregate intrinsic payout to option holders at expiration. The model is deliberately simple; the judgment is in the context around it.

Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, as of the Oct 19, 2026 close. Expiry: Oct 23, 2026 (4 DTE). SPY open interest 778,304 contracts; QQQ 603,734.
Max pain

The payout minimum

For each settlement price S: Call pain(S) = Σ CallOI(K) × max(S − K, 0) × 100 Put pain(S) = Σ PutOI(K) × max(K − S, 0) × 100 Total(S) = Call pain(S) + Put pain(S) Max pain = argmin Total(S)
  • Full chain: every strike with non-zero OI in the Oct 23 expiry is included. The chart is zoomed; the calculation is not.
  • Variants: volume max pain repeats the calculation with Monday’s volume. The blend uses OI + 0.5 × volume.
  • Shelf: every strike whose total payout is within 1% of the minimum. It is a robustness check, not a separate forecast.
  • Payout: intrinsic value at settlement; no discounting, early exercise or assignment frictions.
Volatility & gamma

Implied move and dealer exposure

1σ move = S × IV_ATM × √(DTE / 365) GEX(S) = Σ Γ(K, S) × OI(K) × 100 × S² × 0.01 calls positive · puts negative
  • ATM IV: average of the nearest ATM call and put mid IVs; 1σ uses 4/365 years.
  • Skew: 25Δ risk reversal is 25Δ put IV minus 25Δ call IV. Positive means puts are richer.
  • GEX: Black-Scholes gamma at each hypothetical spot, held OI constant. It is a static, modeled exposure.
  • Sign convention: calls + / puts −. That assumes a particular dealer book; actual positioning is unobservable.

Limitations

What the model cannot see

  • Anonymous OI: customer vs dealer, spreads, hedges and rolls are invisible in end-of-day data.
  • No arbitrage force: max pain is descriptive. Nothing guarantees settlement at the minimum.
  • Static gamma: the model excludes vanna, charm, dividends and intraday hedging. It is a snapshot, not a simulation.
  • Event risk: earnings dates and index weights can change; NFLX weight was unavailable in the data pull.
  • Closing auction: PM settlement and MOC flows can overwhelm the map in the final hour.
  • Quote quality: yfinance IVs are mid-based and can be stale; they are not directly tradeable.

Model outputs are conditional on the current open-interest book and the stated sign convention. A max-pain level is a reference point for positioning, not a price target or a trade recommendation.
