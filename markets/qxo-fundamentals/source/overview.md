## Business

QXO, Inc. operates as a leading publicly traded distributor of roofing, waterproofing, and complementary building products in the United States. The company offers a comprehensive range of products including asphalt, metal, wood, tile, and slate roofing; roofing accessories and insulation products; vinyl, aluminum, steel, fiber cement, wood, and composite siding; trim and gutters with accessories; built-up, modified, EPDM, PVC, and low-slope metal roofing; as well as air and vapor barriers, fluid-applied products, repair and protection solutions, and membrane waterproofing products. Additionally, QXO supplies building materials and tools such as exterior and interior materials and roof hatches, serving contractors, distributors, and suppliers to streamline operations across the construction and building sectors. Founded in 2024 with headquarters located in Greenwich, Connecticut, QXO aims to become the tech-enabled leader in the $800 billion building products distribution industry. The company targets $50 billion in annual revenues over the next decade through a combination of organic growth and accretive acquisitions. A major recent strategic development for QXO includes its April 2025 completion of the approximately $11 billion acquisition of Beacon Roofing Supply, Inc., a transaction that significantly expands its scale and market presence, making QXO the largest publicly traded distributor in its segment in the U.S. This acquisition is part of a broad transformation initiative focused on pricing, procurement, sales, organizational structure, and logistics, designed to boost growth and margins while enhancing customer experience. Beacon is now a wholly owned subsidiary of QXO, with its operations fully integrated. Additional recent company activities include a substantial equity private placement raising $830 million in conjunction with the Beacon acquisition and ongoing strategic initiatives to optimize operational efficiencies and maximize shareholder value. QXO maintains a strong focus on technology integration in its distribution processes and customer solutions, serving a wide geographic footprint across North America. The company's leadership under CEO Brad Jacobs emphasizes leveraging acquisitions and organic initiatives to drive long-term growth in the building products distribution industry. QXO's operations concentrate on customers from contractors to large-scale distributors and suppliers, providing essential building products and service solutions tailored to the construction market. Its broad product portfolio and strategic market position are supported by active investor interest and recent significant institutional investments, reflecting confidence in its growth trajectory and market leadership role. Overall, QXO, Inc. stands out as a major player in building supply distribution, distinguished by its extensive product range, aggressive expansion strategy through acquisitions like Beacon Roofing Supply, and commitment to becoming a technology-driven industry leader headquartered in Greenwich, Connecticut and operating primarily across the United States.
