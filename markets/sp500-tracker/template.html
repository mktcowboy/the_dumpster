Data sources & methodology

Membership: Historical components of the S&P 500, retrieved October 14, 2026; archived revision. Original row citations are preserved. The log records turnover, not a complete membership roster.

Prices: cached monthly unadjusted closes from Yahoo Finance. The latest month may be incomplete. A monthly bar is labeled by its month; the event baseline uses the event month or the first common month afterward. It is not an exact trade at the event date. Returns and annualized returns are calculated from the common comparison window. Annualization uses elapsed calendar months.

A price history beginning more than a year after a ticker’s earliest recorded event is excluded as possible ticker reuse. Missing history does not establish that a company is bankrupt or delisted. Ticker renames and separate membership spells remain separate log events; unknown addition dates remain unknown.

Corrections preserved: Berkshire Hathaway/Burlington Northern Santa Fe, February 16, 2010 (Reuters), and Allegion/JCPenney ticker artifacts, December 2, 2013. Wikipedia coverage is thinner in early years; one-sided entries reflect corporate actions. The earlier audit reported 49 apparent addition-date mismatches resolved as ticker renames or questionable source dates.
