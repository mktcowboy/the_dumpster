# Sociology — SocArXiv Top 20, Sep 18 – Oct 18, 2026 (scout report, received Oct 18, 2026)

## The stats hunt (what exists, what doesn't)
Real stats do exist, but only per-paper. Each OSF preprint page publicly displays "Views: X | Downloads: Y" counters (confirmed via OSF's own docs: https://help.osf.io/article/408-preprint-analytics — counters exclude views/downloads by the paper's own contributors). There is no public ranking or trending page for SocArXiv, and no metrics API: api.osf.io/v2/metrics/... returns 404, v2 preprint records don't carry counters, and the Altmetric API denied access (403) for these DOIs. The SocArXiv blog (socopen.org) only publishes aggregate submission counts, never per-paper leaderboards.

What was done: pulled all 100 SocArXiv preprints created Sep 18–Oct 18, 2026 via the OSF API, then opened each preprint's OSF page and read its counters (stats as of Oct 18, 2026, ~4:30–5pm CT). A 429 rate limit stopped the sweep partway — 65 of 100 papers measured, 35 could not be (mix of pages with no extractable counters and unfetched ones; no retries per hard-stop rule). So this is a top-20 by observed downloads among the 65 measured papers — not a complete ranking, and the true top-20 could differ. Methodology: sorted by downloads descending, ties broken by views descending. Zero editorial curation beyond that. Authors verified per-paper via the OSF citation API (api.osf.io/v2/preprints/{id}/citation/); all 20 URLs are live pages that were successfully fetched.

Biases to know: earlier-in-window papers had more days to accumulate downloads; papers posted Oct 15–16 are systematically disadvantaged. Download counts are small (max 25) because most papers are days old.

## The list
1. [Reassessing the Democratic Advantage in War](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/jvryq_v1/) — Downes & Lyall — Oct 16 — 122 views / 25 downloads. Re-tests the "democracies win wars" claim across 11 democracy measures and the larger Project Mars dataset (490 estimates) and finds no robust advantage — a direct challenge to a canonical IR finding.
   - related: revisits the authors' earlier work: [Downes 2009](https://www.belfercenter.org/publication/how-smart-and-tough-are-democracies-reassessing-theories-democratic-victory-war) (International Security) · [Lyall 2010](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-organization/article/abs/do-democracies-make-inferior-counterinsurgents-reassessing-democracys-impact-on-war-outcomes-and-duration/1B90F5C8769E22F27C8506499931AE14) (International Organization)
2. [Unequal Parenting across Childhood: How Class Differences in Parental Control of Screen Use Reverse with the Child's Age](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/e7qv5_v1/) — Aussant & Roaldsnes — Oct 13 — 25 views / 25 downloads. Norwegian survey of 20,670 households: affluent parents are stricter about screens for young kids but more permissive with teens — class advantage is adaptability, not fixed rules.
3. [Where Silicon Samples Go Wrong: Item-Level Coefficient Distortion in LLM-Simulated Chinese Public Opinion](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/276rd_v2/) — Cui & Xiong — Oct 12 — 5 views / 25 downloads. LLM-simulated Chinese survey responses distort specific coefficients (inflated income gradient on fairness, spurious urban–rural morality gaps) — a caution flag for synthetic public-opinion data.
   - related: concurrent silicon-sampling audit with [European Social Survey data](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16395) (arXiv, Sep 2026)
4. [Political Influencers as Information Sources: Experimental Evidence on Credibility, Belief, and Epistemic Judgment](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/vsy5m_v1/) — Zhu & Merkley — Oct 16 — 3 views / 23 downloads. Canadian experiment: identical political claims lose credibility when attributed to influencers vs. national news; followers' extra belief in false claims mostly washes out after controls.
   - related: [Media Ecosystem Observatory report](https://meo.ca/work/power-shift-the-rise-of-political-influencers-in-canada) on the rise of political influencers in Canada
5. [The Intersection of Fascia, Fascism, and Language: Reframing Neurodivergence as an Adaptive Response to Environmental Stress](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/6vd74_v1/) — Snow — Oct 12 — 26 views / 21 downloads. Provocative theory piece arguing global autism identification rates map social ecology and institutional demands better than biology.
6. [The sociolinguistic labor of incarcerated people](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/h9xg8_v3/) — Licata, Tyler, Coles, et al. — Oct 12 — 6 views / 21 downloads. From 40 interviews with incarcerated students: prison surveillance forces constant "sociolinguistic labor" — code-switching both toward and away from stigmatized speech.
   - flag: first posted Aug 2026 (v3)
7. [Une approche dynamique de la réception des pré-annonces pharmaceutiques dans les réseaux sociaux — Le cas des vaccins contre la Covid-19 sur Twitter](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/huaxq_v1/) — Caron & Benavent — Oct 12 — 5 views / 20 downloads. Francophone Twitter study: pharma efficacy announcements transform debate content instantly, but judgments of the firms shift later (in French).
8. [When Execution Is Cheap: Specification, Evaluation, and Rent Capture in AI-Enabled Work](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/4az6c_v1/) — George — Oct 12 — 8 views / 18 downloads. As AI cheapens execution, value moves to defining outcomes and evaluating outputs — models when that creates economic rents and who captures them.
9. [Passive Behavioral Tracing: A Non-Interventional Fairness Protocol for Cooperative AI](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/up6t5_v1/) — hayashi — Oct 12 — 2 views / 18 downloads. A fairness-monitoring protocol for cooperative AI that needs no new data collection — it works off behavioral traces already being recorded.
10. [An Empirical Study on How Private College Counseling Perpetuates Structural Disadvantage](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/pnjh9_v1/) — ellahi — Oct 12 — 8 views / 17 downloads. Mixed-methods (survey, interviews, social media): private counselors reveal "unspoken" admissions criteria public-school peers never see — 82% of respondents said so.
11. [How Do Unconditional Cash Transfers Break the Poverty Trap? … Lived Experiences in Rural Uganda](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/bp36g_v1/) — Neuwirth, Musanje, Schelleman-Offermans, et al. — Oct 14 — 13 views / 16 downloads. Longitudinal study (n=32, three waves): cash transfers work through a three-stage path — restored cognitive bandwidth, microenterprise startup capital, then psychosocial shifts.
12. [Spiritual Healing Methods in Los Angeles: the Cultural Heritage and Western Representation of Alternative Medicines](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/guzyj_v1/) — Altsuler & Heller — Oct 13 — 2 views / 16 downloads. Six LA practitioners on how Reiki, yoga, and ayahuasca get remade for Western consumer culture.
13. [GLCI (Global Linguistic Connectivity Index): How Connected Is the World?](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/g3vxj_v1/) — Hovhannisyan — Oct 12 — 2 views / 16 downloads. Audits 8.16B people's language repertoires: the chance a random listener understands a random speaker with no translator is just 11.3%, resting mostly on English.
14. [Enhancing Dementia Awareness and Specialized Nurse Training in the Gulf: A Policy Roadmap for Oman, Qatar, UAE, and Saudi Arabia](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/qy7ts_v1/) — Khurana, Kaur, Khan, et al. — Oct 12 — 13 views / 15 downloads. Gulf dementia cases set to more than triple by 2050 while systems skew acute-care — roadmap for accredited nurse specialization.
15. [Climate Change and the Socio-Political Foundations of Welfare](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/u6ptz_v1/) — Zimmermann (author field lists only an email: katharina.zimmermann@uni-hamburg.de) — Oct 12 — 12 views / 15 downloads. Argues 20th-century welfare capitalism worked as "fossil glue" — dependent on growth and cheap energy — and asks whether welfare states can survive ecological constraint.
16. [The Grumpy Meme and Irritability Ecology: How Low-Intensity Negative Affect Spreads, Survives, Sediments, and Becomes a Workplace Climate](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/vg8s9_v1/) — Hiraoka — Oct 12 — 11 views / 15 downloads. Why low-grade grumpiness persists at work: it's cheap, repeatable, hard to sanction, and ambiguity-protected.
17. [The ALPS Index: A Global Estimate of the Average Number of Languages a Person Speaks](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/3mtsx_v1/) — Hovhannisyan — Oct 12 — 2 views / 14 downloads. Same author's companion index: the global average is 1.568 languages per person, and at most 57% of people speak two or more.
18. [What do culture vouchers really buy? Evidence from France's 'pass Culture' policy uses](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/pmdvy_v2/) — Aussant — Oct 14 — 1 view / 14 downloads. Survey of 5,010 young French adults: the €300 pass Culture didn't widen highbrow participation — the money flowed to commercial pop culture.
   - related: France's audit court's [first assessment of pass Culture](https://www.ccomptes.fr/en/publications/first-assesment-pass-culture-scheme) also found spending concentrated in books and cinema
   - flag: first posted Oct 2025 (v2)
19. [Who stops answering? Breakoff, smartphone use and educational bias in a web survey](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/p2c59_v2/) — Ľudmová, Kreidl, Hubatková, et al. — Oct 13 — 9 views / 13 downloads. Czech web survey (N=5,468): the least educated break off at 7% vs 3% for the most educated, worst on smartphones — device explains only part of the gap.
20. [The geopolitics of academic publishing: the case of Kazakhstan](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/48dgy_v1/) — Hartmann & Bektemirova — Oct 12 — 6 views / 13 downloads. How Kazakhstan made Scopus/Web of Science indexing the benchmark of academic excellence to position itself between the West and Russia.

## Caveats and follow-ups
- Partial-stats ranking: 65/100 papers measured. A later run (after the rate limit cools, or via a live-browser delegation with spaced requests) could fill the 35 gaps and re-rank — several unmeasured papers (e.g., the Facebook incumbency-engagement paper, the German CO₂ inequality paper, the Ukraine-aid party-brands paper) are plausible top-20 contenders.
- Subfield mix emerged naturally from the stats: IR/poli sci, family/stratification, methods/AI, comms, sociolinguistics, econ of AI, education, development, culture/religion, demography/language, health policy, environment, orgs, survey methods, science studies.
- The list mixes strong empirical work (1, 2, 3, 11, 19) with more speculative pieces (5, 9, 16) — downloads ≠ quality, judge accordingly.
