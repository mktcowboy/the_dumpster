Sources, method & caveats

Provenance

Every record here comes from Wine Spectator's own Top 100 list explorer, which ships each year's hundred wines inside its JavaScript bundle. This report decodes those files directly, then adds the Top 10 story pages from the editorial site for 2013 onward (see also the archives). For how the editors taste blind and score, see About our tastings.

Each wine links to its Wine Spectator review, where the full tasting note lives; some may need a membership. The Wine-Searcher, Vivino, and CellarTracker links are searches for the winery, wine, and vintage. They usually land on the right bottle but aren't checked matches. This report isn't affiliated with Wine Spectator; the ranks, scores, and prices are theirs.

Notes on the data

    Taster initials

    The source records give initials only. Names are added for the most frequent; the rest are shown as published. Blank means no initials were recorded, which is common before 2000.

    Rebuilding

    python3 fetch.py downloads the source files into a git-ignored .cache/; python3 build.py writes this page plus data/top100.csv and data/top100.json.
