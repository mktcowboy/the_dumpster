How to read this: "Regulatory notes" sum up what applies to the solvent for that kind of food. For rules on the solvent itself, see section 2. The status column separates ingredients sold today (Commercial) from ones a regulation names explicitly (Regulatory-listed/cleared) and ones only seen in studies, pilots or history (Research/Historical). The spice, herb and seed-oil CO₂ rows come from one manufacturer's current catalogue (FLAVEX). Other CO₂ extractors sell the same kinds of products. End-use examples are typical applications.

2. Solvent profiles: rules and residue limits by jurisdiction

EU: Directive 2009/32/EC Annex I. Part I solvents are allowed for all uses under good manufacturing practice (GMP), meaning only technically unavoidable, harmless residues. Parts II and III set specific uses and maximum residues. Codex has no binding extraction-solvent standard, and its Inventory of Processing Aids is informational only. "Not listed" means the solvent is not on that jurisdiction's positive list. In Canada, Australia/NZ and the EU, that generally means it may not be used as an extraction solvent there.
