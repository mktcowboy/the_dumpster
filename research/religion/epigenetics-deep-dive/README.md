# Epigenetics Deep Dive

Long-form, journal-style deep dive on epigenetics (researched Oct 19, 2026):
origins (Waddington 1942 through the 2009 operational definition), the four
core mechanisms (with an SVG methylation-cycle diagram), landmark in-vivo
studies (Agouti mice, Weaver/McGowan maternal care, Dutch Hunger Winter,
honeybees, twins), epigenetic clocks across three generations plus partial
reprogramming, cancer epigenetics with an approved-drugs/pivotal-trials
table and the smoking EWAS, CRISPR epigenome editing through CRISPRoff, the
transgenerational-inheritance debate as case-for vs. case-against with a
bottom-line verdict, myths vs. reality with a five-question critical-reading
checklist, 2023–2026 frontiers (single-cell/spatial epigenomics, ICE-mouse
aging, EPI-321 first-in-human data), and definitive reviews/textbooks.
