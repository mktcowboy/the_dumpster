# References from the water-treatment report

Extracted from `biochar/biochar-activated-carbon-water-report.html`, reference list items 1–25. Authors, year, title, journal or source, and DOI are taken from that list. No authors, volumes, or DOIs were filled in from outside the report.

The quality tier is filled in only when the cite-first shortlist or the papers audit supports it.

- Letter tiers (A or B) match the shortlist row with the same DOI.
- `standard` is used only for the two EPA documents the audit explicitly groups with standards: the 2024 PFAS treatment fact sheet and the 2024 PFAS technologies-and-cost technical support document.
- Everything else stays `not assigned`. The audit did not give those items a letter tier. Notes say when an item is weakly sourced or is official guidance rather than a journal paper.
- None of these 25 items is vendor material. Vendor literature is a lower tier than A or B; see the bibliography README.

DOIs that appear in the shortlist were checked there (OpenAlex, and Crossref where the audit says so), as of November 5, 2026. DOIs that are only in the report were not re-checked for this file. The report says its sources were located on October 22, 2026.
