Data sources & methodology

1
Change log — Wikipedia, “Historical components of the S&P 500”. Live article · Permanent revision (Oct 8, 2026) · retrieved Oct 14, 2026. 392 events, Jan 2007–Oct 2026. Each row carries its own citation: 270 link to the row’s original reference (mostly S&P Dow Jones Indices announcements); the rest cite the Wikipedia table itself. Dates are effective dates as published; announcement and effective dates can differ by a day.
2
Prices — SPY monthly closes, Yahoo Finance, retrieved Oct 14, 2026. 240 months, Nov 2006–Oct 2026 (unadjusted closes).
3
Corrections applied Oct 14, 2026 — (a) Added the missing 2010-02-16 swap: Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.B) replaced Burlington Northern Santa Fe (BNI), which Berkshire acquired — Reuters, Jan 26 2010; (b) fixed ticker artifacts on the 2013-12-02 Allegion/JCPenney row.
4
Completeness audit. Every current constituent’s “Date added” was checked against the log: all 49 apparent misses resolved as ticker renames (EG=RE, META=FB, CPAY=FLT, TT=IR, SATS→ECHO, WRK→SW, DLPH→APTV) or erroneous “Date added” values with no S&P announcement (D, SRE, TROW, HUM, AKAM). The widely reported GE/Walgreens 2018 swap was Dow Jones Industrial Average only, not S&P 500. No other gaps found.

Limitations. Wikipedia’s table is community-maintained and thinner for 2007–2010; a few early entries may be missing. One-sided rows (empty Added or Removed) reflect spin-offs or corporate actions recorded as single-sided changes. S&P occasionally swaps several pairs on one date (quarterly rebalances).
