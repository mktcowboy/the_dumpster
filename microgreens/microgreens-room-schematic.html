Soak + sow→ Stack weighted→ Unstack → lights→ Bottom-water daily→ Cut → pack → fridge

The stagger is the business: sow a new batch every 2–3 days and the room harvests twice a week, every week — no feast-or-famine. Day 0 detail: soak big seeds (sunflower/pea) overnight, fill trays with moist coir, sow dense, mist, cover. Days 1–3: check daily, mist if dry. Days 4–9: 14–16h light, water from below only, fans always on. Harvest at target height, cut above the media, straight into the fridge — never wash.
